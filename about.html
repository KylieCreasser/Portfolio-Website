A little about me

Hi, I’m Kylie.

I’m a marketing student who genuinely enjoys fast-paced, creative work where no two days look exactly the same.

I like both sides of marketing. I love coming up with content that feels natural and on-brand, but I also enjoy looking at the numbers behind it. Reach, timing, format, and performance are just as interesting to me as the creative itself.

I grew up around a family-run business, which taught me a lot about being dependable, listening to people, and building trust over time. At UNC Charlotte, I also competed on the Ski and Snowboard Team while helping coordinate events and fundraising.

I’m now based in Orlando and looking for a team where I can keep learning, contribute creatively, and be someone people can count on.
